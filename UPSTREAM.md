# Reuse of Bitácora Ciber / Bitácora PER

Derived from the user's own Bitácora Ciber (https://github.com/py-qwerty/bitacora-ciberseguridad), itself derived from Bitácora PER. Retained: the dependency-free engine (core.js, app.js), styles, local fonts and test approach. Changed: branding and texts, storage prefix (`bitacora-implantes:v1:`), question sources (`clase`, `practice`), topics may be "en preparación" (0 questions) or complete (30), and the PDF material feature is kept but unused. No question bank, PDFs, campus data or credentials were copied.
